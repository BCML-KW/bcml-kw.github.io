## Research Area Overview

Healthcare is evolving rapidly thanks to connected sensors and AI. Our lab develops applications that merge medical expertise with machine learning so hospitals, clinicians, and patients can access actionable insights anywhere.

### Focus Topics

- Nailfold Capillaroscopy pipelines for early autoimmune and vascular disease diagnosis
- Digital therapeutics and remote-monitoring platforms with explainable, clinician-facing recommendations
- Low-power wearable systems for high-fidelity biosignal sensing and on-device anomaly detection
- Secure IoT backbones that connect patients, clinicians, and decision-support tools
